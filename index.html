The Artisan Standard

Why Our Ice Cream Tastes Unforgettable

Most commercial ice creams are pumped with up to 50% air and cheap hydrogenated oils. At Raj Icecream, we adhere to the ancient European and royal Indian churning traditions.

100% Real Dairy

Unapologetically Rich Dairy

We source fresh, premium whole milk directly from trusted local herds. We never use powdered milk substitutes, synthetic butter essences, or vegetable fats. That thick, silky mouthfeel is 100% pure butterfat from real milk.

  • High butterfat content for velvety glide
  • Absolutely no palm oil or trans-fats
  • Natural golden dairy richness
Botanicals & Fruit

Hand-Roasted Nuts & Real Pulp

Our kitchen hand-dices California almonds, golden cashews, and Iranian pistachios before roasting them in small pans to bring out their essential oils. Fruit varieties only enter our churning vats when they are in their peak harvest season.

  • Real Ratnagiri Alphonso mango pulp
  • Fresh hand-deseeded Sitaphal (Custard Apple)
  • Pure Kashmiri Mongra saffron infusion
Low Overrun

Slow Churned For Dense Luxury

Overrun is the amount of air whipped into ice cream. Factory tubs are up to 60% air. We slow-churn with low overrun, resulting in a dense, custard-like texture that melts slowly and delivers explosive flavor on your palate.

  • Heavy scoop weight with dense body
  • Melts gracefully with no icy crystallization
  • Baked daily fresh cinnamon-vanilla waffle cones
